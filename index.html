About

From purpose to excellence in one learning cycle

Paideia, the Taejae Learning Suite, brings advising, course design, student work and outcomes assessment onto one shared foundation, so evidence of learning no longer breaks between systems.

Students set goals in Telos. Faculty design the course in TaeSylla. Work is shown and rated in Ergon. Arete turns those ratings into course and program outcomes, which flow back to advising and to the next course design.

Paideia means education in its fullest sense, and here it holds the whole cycle.

“Purpose (Telos) guides a well-designed course (TaeSylla), and the course gives shape to the work (Ergon). Through that work excellence (Aretē) is revealed, and it fulfils its purpose (Telos).”Inspired by Aristotle
  • Telosτέλος · purposeAdvising and academic planning

    Students set goals and plan their courses with their advisor. Advisors keep a record of every meeting, and chairs see the whole program.

    Logo: Polaris, the North Star, at the end of the Little Dipper. It keeps a fixed direction, as a purpose does.

    • Advising meetings and logs
    • Booking by office hours
    • Graduation requirements
    • Course plan by term
    • Program view for chairs
    In operation
  • TaeSyllaσίλλυβος · syllabusCourse design and syllabus

    Faculty design each course in one place and publish the syllabus that students read. Chairs keep the curriculum map.

    Logo: Norma, the carpenter’s square. A square sets true lines, as a syllabus sets the shape of a course.

    • Syllabus and schedule
    • CLOs and rubrics
    • Readings and lesson plans
    • Curriculum map
    • Course Finder
    In operation
  • Ergonἔργον · workStudios and showrooms for student and faculty work

    Students and faculty keep their work in studios, from team projects to faculty lectures and special talks, and send it to showrooms. The class or invited guests view it there, and faculty rate student work.

    Logo: the Keystone of Hercules, the hero of the twelve labours. It stands for work that is made and shown.

    • Studios for team projects and faculty lectures
    • Showrooms with dated sessions
    • Invitations for class or guests
    • Rubric rating next to the work
    • Votes and comments
    Pilot · open
  • Areteἀρετή · excellenceLearning outcomes assessment

    Arete turns the ratings from Ergon into course and program outcomes, and keeps the record for accreditation.

    Logo: Corona Borealis, the Northern Crown. A crown honours excellence that has been reached.

    • Rubric to CLO, PLO and ILO
    • Student progress over terms
    • Course reports for faculty
    • Program results for chairs
    • Closing the loop
    Pilot · open
